One picture that tells Thursday 19 November 2026: every main story gets its
own drawing, a strip shows how the day's 41 stories spread across seven
regions, a pinned map shows where they happened, and a date-check box names
the big stories left out on purpose because they broke earlier in the week.

The 41 stories: the first Trump–Xi–Putin meeting in Shenzhen, GTA VI's
worldwide launch, Cyclone Akvan closing on India's east coast, Israeli tanks
pushing deeper into Gaza, NASA's Voyager 1 briefing, India–Japan defence
talks in Tokyo, the deadlocked Gulf attacks, Somalia's charge against the
UAE, a US–Philippines trade deal, Pew's 2035 religion projection, a US
fight over Ukraine aid, Paris police on the march, Deutsche Bank's $582m
Epstein file, Ben Duckett's 133, a stalled COP31 — and 26 briefs.

`poster.html` is the page; headless Chrome prints it to `entry.png`;
`main.py` copies that image to the scorer's output path, byte for byte.
